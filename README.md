# Welcome to FreshStart

## Project info

🌿 FreshStart
Reclaim your time, health, and focus.

FreshStart is a holistic recovery companion designed to help users break free from compulsive behaviors—from substance abuse to digital addictions. By combining evidence-based behavioral strategies with gamified accountability, FreshStart transforms the difficult journey of quitting into a visual growth experience.

🚀 Key Features
-Tailored Recovery Paths: Personalized action plans and science-backed methods for diverse addiction types (Alcohol, Nicotine, Social Media, etc.).

-The Forest (Gamified Sobriety): Your progress is alive. Stay clean to plant seeds and grow a lush, digital forest. Relapses directly affect your ecosystem, providing a powerful visual incentive to stay on track.

-Smart Milestones: Earn badges and track health improvements in real-time as your body and mind recover.

-Crisis Toolkit: Instant access to breathing exercises and cognitive reframing techniques when cravings hit
